Large-eddy simulations at angles of attack of 30° and 50°, over several fineness ratios, test whether aft-body vortex shedding causes the drop in forebody oscillation frequency. The forebody oscillation remains after the aft body is shortened enough that shedding stops. The frequency shift is a property of the forebody vortices. Shortening the aft body lowers their amplitude and frequency only slightly.
