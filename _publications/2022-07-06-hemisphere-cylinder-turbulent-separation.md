Detached-eddy simulation of a hemisphere–cylinder of fineness ratio 24.5 at angles of attack from 30° to 80°, with the Reynolds number based on diameter fixed at 3.0 × 10^6. Below about 45° the wake oscillation is weak and symmetric. Above that angle, slower forebody vortex oscillation and faster aft-body shedding coexist, and the oscillation carries the larger sectional side force.
